The classic direct Intercity runs Rotterdam → Schiedam → Delft → Den Haag HS → Leiden → Schiphol → Amsterdam Zuid (~6 stops, ~60.0 min vs ~33.0 min on the HSL). It is slightly more punctual and far less prone to severe delays, and 39.6% of these originate at Rotterdam (seat). 1st-class emptiness isn't in the open data — that comfort is yours to test — but it also avoids the IC-Direct supplement. Trade ~25 extra minutes for a calmer, more reliable ride.
